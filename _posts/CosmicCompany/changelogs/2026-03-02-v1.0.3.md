# The Company needs you back!
## In the sequel Modpack to "Cosmic Company"

### Welcome employees - to the ship once again, are you brave enough to become the most profitable team in our history?

If so, jump in to "**Cosmic Company Revitalised**"! - A fresh out the oven sequel to "**Cosmic Company**", a Modpack for Lethal Company.

After the release of ***Lethal Company*** **v73** a lot of the game's mods became unstable or depreciated entirely, this made it very difficult to continue updating the original Cosmic Company modpack.
So I made a new version! An even better, stable and performative sequel - including a ton of more features, improvements and tweaks while keeping that same vanilla++ feeling.

## What to expect

**Cosmic Company Revitalized** builds upon ***Lethal Company*** by adding fresh and engaging content that doesn't feel too distant from the base game.

With tons of new **Moons, Interiors, Weather, Scraps** and **Items** there's so much content to indulge in, all tested extensively for great performance and minimal errors.

> *This Modpack is still brand new - there is bound to be some bugs that have*
> *gone undiscovered, but all the big problems have been squashed!*

Due to the new **Dangers** and **Climates** present, employees may have more trouble progressing through their missions, expect increased difficulty - but much higher reward!
